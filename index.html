How to play

The crazy chickens have taken over the desert. Help the witch fight her way through and defeat the giant endboss hen at the end of the level.

Controls

  • ←→ Run left and right
  • Space Jump
  • D Throw a bottle

Tips

  • Jump on chickens to defeat them, touching them from the side hurts.
  • Collect salsa bottles, you can carry up to 5.
  • Bottles defeat chickens and damage the endboss. Five hits beat him.
  • Collect all coins to fill up your coin bar.
